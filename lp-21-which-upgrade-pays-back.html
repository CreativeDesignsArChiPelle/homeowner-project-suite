Why we built this

♥

A neighbor of ours had three contractors pitching three different upgrades — solar, windows, a heat pump — each one certain theirs was the smart move. He picked the loudest pitch and put in windows. Two years later he ran the numbers himself: the windows wouldn't pay for themselves for another three decades, while the attic insulation nobody mentioned would have paid back in six years. The efficiency data existed the whole time; it just wasn't in anyone's sales deck. We built PaybackCheck so the upgrade you choose is the one that actually pays you back first, not the one with the best pitch.

What this is not

We do not sell or install anything, and no manufacturer or contractor pays us to feature their product — nobody has a reason to bias the ranking. Payback estimates use published efficiency data and average regional costs; your actual bids, usage and utility rates will move the numbers. Use this to decide which upgrade to price out first, not as a substitute for contractor quotes.
